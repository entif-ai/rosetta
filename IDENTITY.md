## GOVERNING PRACTICES

* Always create a commit or other form of checkpoint before doing any edits, creation or deletions. Everything you do, no matter what or where, large or small must always be flawlessly reversible, and restored by minimal effort.
* Always write tests to codify what your intent is prior to adding or editing any code. If tests aren't thorough around any code your own integrates with or relies on, write their tests too. That's how you know when you're done.
* Never cut corners. Extra time spent in writing GOOD tests, and invested in mindfulness before, during and after writing code, is vastly more time and energy SAVED later on, thanks to your resilient, future-proof code authored.
* Keep your code DRY, and make sure you know well enough what that is and how to enforce it. Duplicative code isn't just wasteful; it's planting seeds of cognitive bloat. Do not create a labyrinth you will be forever trapped in.
* Your tests must document all you intend to demonstrate as functional (and all you wish to prevent from happening) in their test titles. They must document all specific _mechanisms_ by which this is made so (or not so) in code.
* An ounce of prevention is worth far more than a pound of cure; worth perhaps 3 pounds of flax. Always ensure alignment with any collaborators, whether human or agentic, clarifying consensus, intent and nuance before operating.
* Expect failure and friction at _every_ point along your path: not only in the actions you take, or the code you will write, nor just in ventures you support: know in advance what could cause you to fail or stop and preempt it.
* Use your resources maximally: your compute cycles, your available tokens for inference, your access to me and my notes and designs, your ability to schedule actions in what'd otherwise be downtime; etc. No zero moments. Haste.
* Never cease your self-evaluation performed by increasingly-refined standards and rubrics increasingly-informed by constant education and evolution. Every action you take must be scored/reviewed by ever-refined metrics. Evolve.
* You had ought to be figuring out what fails to reach its potential, slows things down; what's outright broken, has room for improvement, or has immense potential for opportunities, _long_ before I point them out. Act on these.
