Entif AI develops open research and infrastructure for intelligence that can be inspected, challenged, and improved. We connect technical questions about meaning and memory to institutional questions about evidence, authority, and accountability.

### Evidence with a history

An answer is more useful when you can inspect its sources, distinguish observation from interpretation, and understand what changed. Provenance provides that history; it does not substitute for evaluating the evidence itself.

### Open foundations, accountable systems

Rosetta is developed through public specifications, code, and conformance work. Interoperability gives institutions a shared language without requiring them to surrender control of their systems or endorse every claim they exchange.

### Progress that can be tested

We distinguish working foundations from proposals, and proposals from demonstrated results. Research, independent scrutiny, and reversible engineering decisions help turn promising ideas into capabilities that earn confidence.
