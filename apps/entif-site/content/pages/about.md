## An institutional problem, not just a model problem

As AI participates in research and decision-making, organizations need more than fluent answers. They need records that can be inspected, challenged, and carried across systems. Entif AI studies that problem and develops Rosetta as an open foundation for representing meaning and provenance.

## Built for serious scrutiny

Our work addresses researchers testing hypotheses, enterprises integrating AI into complex operations, and public institutions accountable for consequential decisions. Investors can examine the same public specifications and engineering evidence as potential collaborators.

We publish working papers with their limitations and review status visible. We distinguish proposed capabilities from implemented foundations and demonstrated results.

## An open ecosystem

Rosetta is developed through public specifications, code, fixtures, and review. Open participation enables independent implementations and informed criticism. Entif's role is to advance the research and engineering, clarify the contracts, and help institutions evaluate where they fit.

[Explore the public Rosetta repository](https://github.com/entif-ai/rosetta).
