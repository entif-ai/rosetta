## Problem Statement

The Opportunity & Edge Brief is the upstream artifact that feeds the Commitment Canvas. Its purpose is to establish *why we win* (or can plausibly win) and what kind of play this is. It has five fields covering strategic lane, win hypothesis, differentiator map, edge durability estimate, and competitive capital reality. This is the foundational strategic positioning document for every commitment.

## Evidence

From the source PRD:

> "Purpose: establish why we win (or can plausibly win) and what kind of play this is."

## Five Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Strategic lane (required, pick at least one) | Create a new space / disrupt existing space by doing it better / enhance existing contested space with major differentiator / relieve a major pain point |
| Win hypothesis | One paragraph: "We win because…" |
| Differentiator map | Product capability, execution velocity, distribution/access, economics/cost curve, trust/brand, posture/regulatory strategy, data/feedback advantage |
| Edge durability estimate | What causes edge to decay: copyability, incumbents, platform shifts, data commoditization, regulation |
| Competitive capital reality | Who can outspend/outwait/out-distribute; where to avoid head-to-head contests without superiority |

## Questions to Resolve Before Implementation

1. Does the Strategic Lane selection gate the commitment canvas, or can multiple lanes be selected?
2. What is the format for the differentiator map? A table? A list? A weighted score?
3. How does this interact with existing competitive analysis artifacts or research?
4. Is "win hypothesis" a falsifiable statement or a narrative? Should it be tied to the Evidence Ledger?
5. How often should the Opportunity & Edge Brief be updated — at each commitment tranche, quarterly, on major market changes?
