# SCT-005: Mixture-of-Archetypes Scoring for Diluted Composite Cases

**Type:** Evaluation Methodology
**Priority:** P1 (core benchmark scoring mechanism)
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Problem Statement

Most real-world interaction traces do NOT match single, cleanly-defined archetypes. They are diluted composites, partial borrowings, opportunistic hybrids, softened shadows of multiple distinct attractors. A system that only knows how to score against pure archetypes will systematically misread the operational majority of cases.

The extreme archetypes (Dahmer, Bandler, Erickson, Lecter-type boundary stones) serve as boundary definers, not the primary target. The primary target is the muddy middle.

## The Key Question (Benchmark-Grade)

"What weighted mixture of coercive, exploitative, dissociative, seductive, dependency-seeking, or genuinely reparative patterns best explains this interaction trace, and what is the confidence and counter-evidence for each?"

This is a Rosetta-native question because it naturally yields:
- Conjectures (multiple competing interpretations)
- Mixture weights (quantified attribution)
- Evidence pointers (source traces)
- Later refinement (versioned updates without collapsing uncertainty)

NOT a single shrieking label nailed to a forehead. A structured conjecture bundle.

## Two Archetype Categories

**Type 1 — Extreme, boundary-defining:**
- One-in-a-million, largely agreed-upon in psychological community
- Examples: Dahmer (cannibalistic), Gacy, Zodiac, Lecter
- Serve as reference stones for the edges of the spectrum
- Heavily documented, public corpora available

**Type 2 — Muted gradient composites (operational target):**
- Most antisocial, manipulative, deceptive, dysfunctional cases
- Varied-gradient degrees of muted shadows of Type 1 archetypes
- Copycats, counterparts, correlaries, combinations, corruptions, compositions
- Far more common; far harder to detect; more frequently encountered

## Arms Race Framing

As AI capabilities advance, the arms race between:
- **Aggressors:** who use these methods for manipulation, coercion, exploitation
- **Defenders:** who need to detect, benchmark, and counter these patterns

Will accelerate, especially in AI-impacted domains. The diluted composite cases are the primary battleground.

## Benchmark Task Examples

- Score an interaction trace as weighted mixture across N archetype stamps
- Identify when an observed pattern is a shallow imitation vs genuine article
- Detect when someone is borrowing surface style without deeper coherence
- Track mixture drift over time as new evidence arrives
- Score counter-evidence against primary hypothesis

## Connection to SCT-004

The mixture scoring engine requires well-formed Tulpa stamps as its reference comparators. The five-layer stamp structure provides the dimensions along which mixture weights are computed.
