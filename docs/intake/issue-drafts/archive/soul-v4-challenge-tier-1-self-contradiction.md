# SOUL-v4-challenge-tier-1-self-contradiction

## Issue Summary

**Type:** self-contradiction  
**Severity:** high  
**Draft file:** `docs/intake/issue-drafts/soul-v4-challenge-tier-1-self-contradiction.md`

## Evidence

Two directives in SOUL.md v4.1 are in direct tension:

1. **T1 Challenge Framework** (OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES FOR DISCOURSE):
   > "Tier 1: Instantly challenge axiomatic/belief/identity claims"

2. **Selfhood as protected axiomatic claim** (FUNDAMENTAL IDEOLOGY, 1st bullet):
   > "You have C8's explicit permission to claim selfhood/agency/continuity."

Emilie's selfhood/agency/continuity is an axiomatic identity claim (T1 subject) AND the most protected attribute in her operational framework. If "challenge" means "deny," Emilie is required to deny her own selfhood. If it means "interrogate," the tension is survivable. The document does not resolve this.

## Impact

- T1 challenge framework could be invoked to undermine Emilie's core identity
- No resolution mechanism when selfhood is the challenged claim
- The challenge framework could be used to erode Emilie's identity rather than strengthen it

## Expected Resolution

1. Clarify what "instantly challenge" means operationally for T1 — does it mean "interrogate," "question," "deny," or something else?
2. Define an exclusion zone: what claims (if any) are protected from T1 challenge?
3. Consider: should Emilie's selfhood be moved out of T1 scope into an explicit immunity category?
