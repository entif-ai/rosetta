## Summary

SAFE (Shared Asset Framework / Ecosystem) is the coordination layer that bridges Villaticus's physical exchange lanes and prevents them from collapsing into chaos. When physical exchanges are re-established, the hard problems become custody, condition, trust, security, and logistics — SAFE owns those.

## Synthesis Framing

"SAFE is the coordination layer that keeps analog lanes from collapsing into chaos. Once you stop needing to search for inventory, the hard problems stop being 'search' and become custody/condition/trust/security/logistics. That's the same pattern whether you're lending kids' clothes or distributing scarce tickets."

## Villaticus Ledger Interface Requirements

From synthesis, Villaticus Needs & Offers Ledger must provide:
- open, modular processes
- local-first decisions
- privacy/no-surveillance invariants
- non-capturable commons
- "library principle" (shared access beating ownership; idle capacity utilization)

## SAFE Core Responsibilities (in Villaticus context)

1. **Custody** — who holds items between exchange
2. **Condition** — item state tracking at handoff
3. **Trust** — reputation without algorithmic mediation (Dunbar-scale)
4. **Security** — physical安全 without surveillance capitalism
5. **Logistics** — scheduling, pickup, delivery for in-person exchanges

## Recommended Action

1. Integrate Villaticus physical allocation (ESA-004) with SAFE coordination
2. Define Needs & Offers Ledger as SAFE interface for Villaticus
3. Ensure privacy/no-surveillance invariants are enforced at SAFE layer
4. Implement "library principle" shared access model in SAFE
