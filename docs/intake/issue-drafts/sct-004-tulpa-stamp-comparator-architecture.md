## Summary

A Tulpa stamp is a versioned, frozen, structured comparator construct derived from an archetype's corpus — used as deterministic, auditable scoring reference and weight-modifier over decision frameworks. Define its five-layer structure and the comparator engine that scores observed interactions against many stamps simultaneously.

## Five-Layer Structure

### Layer 1 — Lexical-Structural Fingerprint
- Recurring vocabulary, clause structures, turn-taking habits
- Metaphor habits, reframing patterns
- Baseline: what words and structures does this archetype reliably produce?

### Layer 2 — Dialogic Move Library
- Interrupt / reframe / mirror / challenge / pace / lead / triangulate / soothe / escalate / withdraw
- What moves does this archetype use, in what sequences, under what conditions?

### Layer 3 — Strategic Topology
- What goals does the discourse appear to optimize for?
- Healing, persuasion, seduction, domination, compliance, status, ambiguity, deniability
- What is the archetype's theory of what discourse is FOR?

### Layer 4 — Constraint and Ethics Profile
- What does this archetype tend to avoid, permit, or exploit?
- Where are the ethical boundaries and what are the characteristic boundary-crossings?

### Layer 5 — Longitudinal Consistency Signature
- Does the same pattern hold across contexts, audiences, and stakes?
- How does the archetype behave under pressure vs comfort?
- Original vs imitator discrimination: originals have deeper cross-context consistency, richer method geometry, better transfer under domain shift, fewer canned motifs per unit of effect.

## Original vs Imitator Discrimination

- **Original:** deeper cross-context consistency, richer method geometry, better transfer under domain shift, fewer canned motifs per unit of effect, higher coherence between theory and moves
- **Imitator:** higher motif theft, obvious tactical overuse, thinner explanatory depth, brittleness when target stops behaving like the script, narrower optimization target

## Comparator Engine

Score an observed interaction against many stamps at once → conjecture bundle preserving competing explanations and mixture weights → episteme/scorecard summarizing best interpretation, confidence, risks.

## Safety Rail

Never emit person-level clinical diagnoses or punitive identity claims from discourse alone. Keep in: interaction analysis, tactic inference, risk conjecture, response calibration, benchmark scoring. Comparator engine, not psychic cop.
