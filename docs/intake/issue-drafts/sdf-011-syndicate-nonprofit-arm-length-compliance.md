# SDF-011: For-Profit Syndicate Vendor Relationships with Nonprofits — Arm's-Length Compliance

## Status
draft

## Type
governance

## Summary
Document the arm's-length vendor compliance requirements for transactions between Crates's for-profit syndicate (Hosted.Domains, Sppt.Tech, Dev.Cash, etc.) and the nonprofit entities (Apotheosis Church, VieDay, Villaticus) under the fiscal sponsorship structure.

## Problem

Nonprofits can buy services from founder-owned for-profits, but IRS prohibits private inurement and impermissible private benefit. This is the "easiest attack surface on your exemption" according to prior analysis. Example: Hosted.Domains providing servers/hosting for VieDay app, community, and website.

## Required Compliance Mechanics

### Written Conflict-of-Interest Policy
- Church and project boards adopt formal COI policy
- Directors/officers with financial interest in vendor companies must disclose
- Recusal procedure: interested party does not vote on or approve related transactions

### Board-Level Approval
- All vendor contracts approved by nonprofit board with interested party recused
- Approval documented in minutes with specific discussion of fair market value

### Fair Market Value Documentation
- Obtain comparable bids or rate cards from unrelated vendors
- Document pricing analysis in board record
- Rate card or pricing schedule attached to contract
- Annual review of FMV as market changes

### Arm's-Length Contract
- Written contract that looks like any vendor agreement
- No unusual terms, no sweetheart pricing beyond documented FMV
- Payment terms standard (net 30, etc.)
- No side letters or undisclosed arrangements

### Monitoring and Reporting
- Annual review of vendor relationship
- Conflict-of-interest annual statement from all board members
- Fiscal sponsorship grant reporting includes vendor transaction disclosures

## Specific Transactions (Known)

- Hosted.Domains → VieDay: server and hosting services for app/community/website
- Sppt.Tech → VieDay: Telecom/PBX/AI Voice services (potential)
- Crates.Media → Apotheosis Church: content production, distribution services
