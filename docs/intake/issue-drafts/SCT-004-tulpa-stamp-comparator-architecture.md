# SCT-004: Tulpa Stamp Five-Layer Comparator Architecture

**Type:** Architecture / Specification
**Priority:** P1 (core differentiator for archetype-based evaluation)
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Problem Statement

The Tulpa stamp concept needs rigorous definition to avoid two failure modes:
1. **Over-interpretation** (treating it as a fake person in a jar, full synthetic personhood clone)
2. **Under-interpretation** (treating it as merely a static fingerprint, losing the structural richness)

Crates clarified emphatically: a Tulpa stamp IS a bounded, instantiated, interactable simulacrum of the source archetype — but the right kind. NOT persona theater, NOT voice cosplay. A versioned, structured comparator construct used as deterministic, auditable scoring reference.

## Five-Layer Structure

A Tulpa stamp must contain at minimum:

1. **Lexical-structural fingerprint**
   - Recurring vocabulary and vocab entropy
   - Clause structures and clause-length distribution
   - Turn-taking habits
   - Metaphor habits and metaphorical density
   - Reframing patterns and reframing-to-response ratio

2. **Dialogic move library**
   - interrupt / reframe / mirror / challenge / pace / lead / triangulate / soothe / escalate / withdraw
   - Move sequencing and transition probabilities
   - Response latency signatures

3. **Strategic topology**
   - What goals the discourse appears to optimize for:
     - Healing, persuasion, seduction, domination, compliance, status, ambiguity, deniability, repair, alliance-maintenance
   - Objective function approximation
   - Goal hierarchy

4. **Constraint and ethics profile**
   - What the archetype tends to avoid
   - What the archetype permits
   - What the archetype exploits
   - Red lines and violation responses

5. **Longitudinal consistency signature**
   - Whether the same pattern holds across contexts, audiences, stakes
   - Transfer robustness under domain shift
   - Coherence between stated theory and actual moves

## Key Distinction: Original Innovator vs Imitator

**Original innovator (Bandler, Erickson, Satir):**
- Deeper cross-context consistency
- Richer underlying method geometry
- Better transfer under domain shift
- Fewer canned motifs per unit of effect
- Higher coherence between stated theory and actual moves

**Imitator (Mystery, Ross Jeffries, Trump, PUA ecosystem):**
- Higher motif theft
- More obvious tactical overuse
- Thinner explanatory depth
- Brittleness when target stops behaving like the script
- Narrower optimization target

"The real architect built a cathedral; the imitator sells stained-glass stickers."

## Safety Rail

**Never let the system emit person-level clinical diagnoses or punitive identity claims from discourse alone.**
Keep in: interaction analysis, tactic inference, risk conjecture, response calibration, benchmark scoring. Comparator engine, NOT psychic cop.
