## Problem Statement

The "Treating Trends Tantamount to Trading Technicals" chat proposes a novel discipline: treating cultural trends, content metadata, and influencer dynamics as financial instruments with full multi-dimensional vector spaces, graph databases, bio-inspired algorithms, and AI-driven forecasting.

Crates explicitly claims this is novel IP: *"I just invented it."* ChatGPT confirmed no known prior art matches the specific framing.

Before committing roadmap resources, the novelty claim must be validated.

---

## Proposed Investigation

1. **Academic literature search**: Query relevant academic databases for papers combining memetic analysis with financial market models. Search terms: "attention as capital", "cultural market analysis", "memetic financial modeling", "social media trend prediction financial models", "influencer valuation analytics".

2. **Competitive landscape scan**: Identify existing platforms or services that treat influencers as equities or apply financial analytics to social media growth. Examples to check: BuzzSumo, Tubular Labs, PeckShield, SimilarWeb,InfluencerDB, HypeAuditor.

3. **Patent search**: Check whether any patents cover the specific combination of (a) treating social platforms as exchanges, (b) influencers as equities, (c) content characteristics as technical indicators, and (d) bio-inspired algorithms for trend discovery.

4. **OSS / research project scan**: Check GitHub and research repositories for projects combining graph databases + trend analysis + financial-style forecasting for social media.
