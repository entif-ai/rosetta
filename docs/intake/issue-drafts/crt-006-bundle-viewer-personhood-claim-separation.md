# CRT-006: Bundle Viewer — Separate Content Authenticity from Personhood Authenticity

## Meta

- **Type:** implementation
- **Severity:** high
- **Confidence:** high
- **Tags:** bundle-viewer, personhood-provenance, claim-separation, UX, viewer, rpp
- **Source doc:** `docs/ideas/Chat GPT - LLM Reasoning Theory.md` (Governance Addendum §4, §12)
- **Extracted:** 2026-06-01

## Summary

Bundle viewers and downstream consumers must display content authenticity and personhood authenticity as distinct claim layers. A "verified content" label must never be presented as equivalent to "verified personhood." The viewer must support distinguishing: authentic person-originated content, system-generated ordinary content, system-generated calibrated on a person corpus, simulation-risk content, and contested identity claims.

## Evidence

From Governance Addendum §4 (Separation of Content and Personhood Claims):

> A compliant implementation MUST treat content authenticity, authorship attribution, identity continuity, and simulation disclosure as distinct claim classes.
> A verifier, viewer, or bundle consumer MUST NOT treat proof that content was generated by a known system, pipeline, or signer as equivalent to proof that a represented person genuinely authored, endorsed, or behaved in accordance with that content.

From §12 (Viewer/Verifier Obligations):

> A compliant viewer... MUST be able to distinguish, where applicable:
> - authentic person-originated content
> - system-generated content with ordinary provenance
> - system-generated content calibrated on a person corpus
> - simulated or impersonation-risk content
> - and unresolved or contested identity claims
> A compliant implementation MUST NOT collapse these states into a single "verified" or "authentic" indicator.

## Required Claim States

The viewer must represent at minimum these five states separately:

| State | Label suggestion | Meaning |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| Person-originated | "Verified Person" | Genuine human authored, continuity proven |
| System ordinary | "System Provenance" | Generated by known system/pipeline, no personhood claim |
| Person-calibrated | "Person-Informed" | Shaped by person corpus but not impersonating |
| Simulation-risk | "Identity Risk" | Style/likelihood close enough to warrant disclosure |
| Contested | "Unresolved Identity" | Identity claim disputed or unverified |

## Implementation Requirements

### Viewer Data Model
- Bundle must carry identity-sensitive metadata alongside content provenance metadata
- When identity-sensitive receipts are present (CRT-003), viewer must render the associated claim state
- No fallback to single "verified" indicator when identity claims are present

### UX Design
- Clear visual distinction between content-provenance badge and personhood-provenance badge
- Identity risk state must be prominent, not hidden in "more info"
- Simulation disclosure must be surfaced when present
- Unresolved identity claims must not be silently upgraded to "verified"

### Interoperability
- Downstream consumers (bundle parsers, third-party verifiers) must receive structured identity claim data, not just a boolean

## Dependencies

- CRT-003 (receipt vocabulary): viewer renders the typed receipts defined there
- CRT-005 (default-deny): without default-deny, identity-risk content could enter the bundle without disclosure

## Response Options

1. **Viewer extension in existing Bundle Builder** — Add identity-claim rendering layer to current viewer implementation; lowest cost
2. **New identity provenance viewer component** — Build a dedicated personhood-provenance viewer as a separate module that composes with existing viewer; cleaner separation
3. **Design spec first, implementation second** — Define the 5-state model formally before implementation; coordinate with UX research
