# GAP-003: Viewer Collapses Content Authenticity and Personhood Authenticity

**Priority:** P2
**Status:** draft
**Source:** docs/governance/rosetta_governance_addendum_personhood_provenance_and_cognitive_twin_risk.md §Technical Control Objective 1; §Viewer and Verifier Obligations

## Problem

The addendum's §12 (Viewer and Verifier Obligations) explicitly requires that a compliant viewer MUST be able to distinguish five distinct states:
1. authentic person-originated content
2. system-generated content with ordinary provenance
3. system-generated content calibrated on a person corpus
4. simulated or impersonation-risk content
5. unresolved or contested identity claims

The addendum further states: "A compliant implementation MUST NOT collapse these states into a single 'verified' or 'authentic' indicator."

The addendum's §4 (Separation of Content and Personhood Claims) reinforces this at the normative level: "A verifier, viewer, or bundle consumer MUST NOT treat proof that content was generated by a known system, pipeline, or signer as equivalent to proof that a represented person genuinely authored, endorsed, or behaviorally originated that content."

Current bundle viewers expose a single content-authenticity indicator. A viewer receiving a bundle with a valid content provenance receipt and a high-fidelity simulation of a living person would display both as equivalently "verified" — a false equivalence with significant deception risk.

## Required Action

Extend bundle viewer to expose distinct display layers for:
- content provenance (what system/pipeline generated this)
- authorship evidence (who or what authored this)
- identity continuity evidence (is the represented person genuinely present)
- simulation disclosure (was this calibrated on or simulating a real person)
- unresolved uncertainty or contestability

The viewer must present these as visually and semantically distinct states. A bundle with a valid content receipt but no identity continuity evidence must not display a personhood "verified" indicator.

Update bundle schema to carry personhood provenance sections per §4 requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Viewer can display five distinct state indicators for identity-related provenance
- Viewer does not collapse simulation or impersonation-risk content into a generic "verified" indicator
- Viewer exposes unresolved identity uncertainty rather than resolving it silently
- Bundle schema supports personhood provenance sections with separate content/authorship/identity/simulation/uncertainty fields

## Notes

This is primarily a viewer/UI concern but requires coordination with the bundle schema to carry the necessary identity-provenance fields. GAP-002 (receipt types) and GAP-001 (Guard classification) are prerequisites — without the typed receipt infrastructure, the viewer has no identity-provenance data to display.
