# ACM-007: Design Partner Belief Pack — Five Artifact Deliverables

## Issue

The go-to-market strategy identified in the API-driven cache management dialogue requires five specific belief-pack artifacts before active outreach to the warm network:

1. 10-12 slide executive deck
2. 1-page executive memo
3. 1-page security/governance posture document
4. 1-page design-partner offer
5. 1-page ROI frame (token savings + privacy boundary reduction + governance simplification)

Without these, the warm network (C-level execs at 9-figure ARR startups, SVPs at Fortune 100s) has no artifact to circulate internally after the meeting. Sales cycles stall at the "that was interesting, what do I do with that?" point.

## Scope

1. **Executive deck (10-12 slides)**: Situation (enterprise AI adoption blockers), problem (token burn, governance gaps, privacy exposure, vendor lock-in), solution (Entif as inference firewall + semantic cache router), proof points (handbook workflow example, cost reduction trajectory), call to action (design partner cohort).

2. **Executive memo (1 page)**: Condensed version of deck in memo format. For execs who read before meetings. Must be scannable in < 2 minutes.

3. **Security/governance posture (1 page)**: NOT a marketing document. A precise articulation of Entif's security properties: on-prem boundary, Guard as PEP, ABAC/RBAC before cache lookup, receipts for all durable mutations, immutable audit trail, Tripwire as kill switch. This is what the buyer's security team will ask for.

4. **Design partner offer (1 page)**: What a design partner gets (priority access, direct roadmap influence, architecture workshop + pilot scoping, commercial conversion path). What Entif asks for (engagement fee, feedback loop, referenceable outcome). Clean, concrete, no ambiguity on scope.

5. **ROI calculator (1 page or spreadsheet)**: Input: current monthly token spend on AI workflows. Output: estimated Entif-path spend, estimated privacy exposure reduction, estimated governance improvement index. The handbook workflow as the worked example. Buyers want their own numbers, not our numbers.
