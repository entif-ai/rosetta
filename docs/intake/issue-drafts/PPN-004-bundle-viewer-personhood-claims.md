## Problem Statement

The bundle viewer currently has no concept of personhood provenance as distinct from content provenance. It cannot represent the five layers required by Section 4 and Section 12, and risks collapsing them into a single "verified" or "authentic" indicator — which would directly violate the normative specification's separation requirement.

## Evidence

Section 4 "Separation of Content and Personhood Claims":

> "A compliant implementation MUST treat content authenticity, authorship attribution, identity continuity, and simulation disclosure as distinct claim classes. A verifier, viewer, or bundle consumer MUST NOT treat proof that content was generated by a known system, pipeline, or signer as equivalent to proof that a represented person genuinely authored, endorsed, or behaviorally originated that content."

Section 12 "Viewer and Verifier Obligations" requires viewers to distinguish:
1. Authentic person-originated content
2. System-generated content with ordinary provenance
3. System-generated content calibrated on a person corpus
4. Simulated or impersonation-risk content
5. Unresolved or contested identity claims

## Required Actions

1. Extend bundle schema to support a personhood provenance layer alongside content provenance
2. Update bundle viewer UI/UX to display 5-state provenance display
3. Implement the "collapse prevention" rule: viewer must never render a single "verified" indicator that combines content and personhood provenance
4. Add uncertainty/contestability indicator for unresolved identity claims
5. Add simulation disclosure indicator where applicable
6. Write conformance tests verifying the 5-state display and collapse prevention

## Five-Layer Display Specification

| Layer | Display When | Risk If Conflated |
|---|---|---|
| Content provenance | Always | Low |
| Authorship evidence | When authorship attribution is present | Medium |
| Identity continuity evidence | When identity continuity is claimed | High |
| Simulation disclosure | When content was simulated/calibrated | High |
| Unresolved uncertainty | When identity claims are contested | Critical |

## Dependencies

- PPN-001: Governance domain must define what counts as identity-sensitive
- PPN-002: RRP identity receipt families needed to populate the personhood layer
- PPN-003: Guard-layer must route identity-sensitive ops before viewer can display results
- PPN-014: Selective disclosure mechanism needed to protect person-model internals in viewer
