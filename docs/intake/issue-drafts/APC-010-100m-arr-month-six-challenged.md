# APC-010: $100M ARR by Month Six Is Champagne Hallucination — Category Winability Affirmed

**Status:** draft
**Priority:** medium
**Type:** business/strategy
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Problem Statement

Crates stated that "a finished version of this for a qualified sales juggernaut would put us at $100m ARR by month SIX."

This projection is directly challenged in the conversation as "champagne hallucination."

**The challenge is not against the product thesis. The challenge is against the speed-of-revenue fantasy.**

## Why the Timeline Is Unrealistic

Enterprise sales characteristics that defeat fast revenue:
- Procurement cycles for infrastructure/security products are 6–18 months
- Security review for a new AI vendor is a multi-team, multi-round process
- The buyers who care most (regulated industries: banking, healthcare, legal) are also the most rigorous in proof requirements
- Paid design partner engagements and pilots are the norm before SaaS contracts — these take time to structure and execute

## What IS Affirmed

The category thesis is affirmed as strong:

> "This could plausibly become a $100m+ ARR category winner, but only if you first dominate a painfully specific wedge and prove measurable savings plus measurable risk reduction."

The product category is real and differentiated:
- "AI governance and reasoning infrastructure for enterprises that want the value of LLMs without surrendering privacy, spend discipline, access control, or auditability."

## The Correct GTM Move

Not "wait until the product is ready."

Rather: **parallelize build + market, but constrain the first sale to a specific wedge.**

Specific wedges identified:
1. **Secure employee-policy / handbook / benefits / knowledge workflows** — measurable: lower latency, lower cost, better auditability; demo-able without full Entif deployment
2. **Secure engineering context fabric and cache governance for agentic coding** — one repo, deterministic pack, provider caching, instrumented hit rates
3. **Secure retrieval and synthesis across internal systems with rights-aware composition**

The "sell before build" approach is validated: artifacts first, pilot structure, then build against demand signal.

## Requirements

- Do not use $100M/6-month projection in formal materials — it undermines credibility with sophisticated buyers
- Use category-winability framing instead: "This category is worth $100M+ ARR; our path is [specific wedge] → [measurable proof] → [scale]"
- Lead with a specific wedge (handbook example), not the full Entif vision
- Build MVP that serves the sales motion, not the other way around
