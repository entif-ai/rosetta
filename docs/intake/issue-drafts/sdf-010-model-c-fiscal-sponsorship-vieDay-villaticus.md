# SDF-010: Model C Fiscal Sponsorship Structure for VieDay/Villaticus

## Status
draft

## Type
governance

## Summary
Document the Model C (pre-approved grant) fiscal sponsorship structure for VieDay and Villaticus under Apotheosis Church, ensuring donor contributions flow through the church with documented discretion/control and not as a mere conduit.

## Structure

### Church (Sponsor)
- Legal recipient for donations
- Holds 501(c)(3) automatic exemption
- Responsible for grants to sponsored projects
- Retains discretion and control over all funds

### VieDay / Villaticus (Sponsored Projects)
- Separate legal entities (nonprofit corporations being formed)
- Own bank accounts for operations
- Own boards, missions, accounting when mature
- Receive grants from church under written grant agreement

### Donation Flow
1. Donor contributes to church (earmarked "for VieDay")
2. Church receipts donation as 501(c)(3) charitable contribution
3. Church makes grant to VieDay project under pre-approved grant agreement
4. Monitoring and reporting per grant agreement

### Critical Constraint
Church MUST NOT be a "mere conduit" — automatic passthrough of donations to VieDay/Villaticus without discretion/control violates IRS rules and risks exemption.

## Required Documents

- Fiscal sponsorship agreement (church ↔ project) with: project description, grant amount/rate, reporting requirements, church discretion clause, dissolution clause
- Donor acknowledgment language: "Your contribution is to Apotheosis Church for the benefit of [Project]. Church retains discretion and control."
- Grant agreements (one per project) with milestones, reporting, use restrictions
- Project board minutes / governance structure

## Exit Plan

When VieDay/Villaticus obtain their own 501(c)(3) determinations:
1. Project transitions to independent operation
2. Church grants final uncommitted funds to project
3. Fiscal sponsorship agreement terminated
4. Project operates independently under own determination
