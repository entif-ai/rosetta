## Conceptual Claims

- Consumer UWB ranging + multi-radio swarm can achieve behavioral geometry mapping (crowd flow, occupancy, relative topology) but not radar-grade 3D reconstruction without dedicated infrastructure
- Phase-coherent virtual array from consumer phones is not achievable; multi-device adds constraint density, not coherence
- CMU DensePose-from-WiFi demonstrates learned CSI→pose mapping is feasible under same-layout assumption; domain generalization is the hard unsolved problem
- "Ephemeral Panopticon" (know what's happening without storing who did it) is the correct design pattern for privacy-preserving venue security
- Guardian Mode is Tripwire Protocol extended to spatial incidents, not a new invention
- Church automatic 501(c)(3) exemption enables speed-to-operate for nonprofit accelerator structure
- Model C fiscal sponsorship enables VieDay/Villaticus to operate under church umbrella while pursuing independent determinations
- For-profit syndicate as vendor to nonprofits requires arm's-length discipline; private inurement/impermissible private benefit is primary compliance attack surface
- Audio TDOA is viable with anchor time-pilots and continuous drift modeling; naive waveform correlation fails in reverberant multi-source environments

## Dependencies And Sequencing

- SDF-GM implementation depends on: anchor deployment (surveyed positions), OS-level measurement access (UWB ranging API, WiFi CSI if available), factor graph estimator, quorum protocol
- Church formation precedes fiscal sponsorship of VieDay/Villaticus; church must be operational (real services, real community) to support 501(c)(3) qualification

## Contradictions Or Supersession

- Earlier ChatGPT response ("you can't do imaging from phones alone") later acknowledged and refined: swarm improves localization and constraint density significantly, just not coherence; corrected in subsequent turns
- CMU "fine-grain pose from WiFi" initially overstated by ChatGPT as "achieves astonishing speed and precision"; later corrected to "learned inference under same-layout assumption, drops on unseen layouts"
